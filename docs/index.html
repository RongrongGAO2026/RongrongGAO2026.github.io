About

I am an AI researcher working on 3D vision, multimodal learning, generative AI, and robotic perception. I received my PhD in Computer Science and Engineering from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), under the supervision of Prof. Qifeng Chen.

My research explores how intelligent systems can perceive, reconstruct, and reason about the physical world. It spans geometric scene understanding, RGB-D representation learning, point-cloud processing, generative-model watermarking, and efficient multimodal-to-3D systems for embedded hardware.

Before and during my doctoral work, I developed applied perception systems at SenseTime, Allybot, and HKUST, covering autonomous driving, camera-LiDAR fusion, real-time inference, and end-to-end sensing-to-learning pipelines.
